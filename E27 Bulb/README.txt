Summary:
This is a very dangerous build due to the high voltage involved. You have to know the dangers and potential issues: Short-circuits and loose wires. This build violates any good electrician guidelinesA lamp or bulb holder designed for the iDual LED bulb by Müller Licht or Jedi Lighting (http://www.mueller-licht.de/produktinformationen/artikel/400009/). But should fit any LED bulb. It can stand on a table or be mounted on a wall by a screw/nail. It is meant to provide electricity for the bulb by electrical contacting. Only for LED bulbs! No tungsten bulbs, these will melt the mount immediately. The cooling fins of the LED I use reach up to 70°C. But the screw base of the bulb is only below 30°C. The mount does not deform or melt.You will need two 10mm M3 screws and four M3 nuts, two paper-clips for inner contacting, a cable tie and a wire with a plug.As always a STEP file is included for easy changes and adjustments.
